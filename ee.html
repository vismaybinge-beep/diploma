electronical and electronic ENGINEERING

INTRODUCTION

Mastering electrical and electronics starts with understanding fundamental laws and core components. Key concepts revolve around voltage (electrical pressure), current (flow of electrons), and resistance (opposition). Together, these enable the building blocks of circuits, power generation, and signal processing.Fundamental Electrical EngineeringOhm's Law: The backbone of circuit analysis, defining the relationship between voltage (V), current (I), and resistance (R). The formula is V = I × R.Kirchhoff's Laws:Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL): The total current entering a junction or node equals the total current leaving it.Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL): The algebraic sum of all voltages in any closed loop is zero.Circuit Configurations:Series: Components are connected end-to-end. Current is the same through all components, but voltage is divided. Total resistance: \(R_{total} = R_1 + R_2 + R_3\).Parallel: Components share the same two nodes. Voltage is the same across all components, but current is divided. Total resistance: \(\frac{1}{R_{total}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3}\).Passive Circuit ComponentsResistors (R): Opposes the flow of current. Unit: Ohms (Ω).Capacitors (C): Stores energy in an electric field. Unit: Farads (F).Inductors (L): Stores energy in a magnetic field. Unit: Henrys (H).AC vs. DC FundamentalsDirect Current (DC): Current flows in one continuous direction. Common sources include batteries.Alternating Current (AC): Current periodically reverses direction. In India, the mains power grid operates at a frequency of 50 Hz.Power (P): The rate of energy transfer, calculated as P = V × I. Unit: Watts (W).Semiconductors & ElectronicsDiodes: Semiconductor devices that allow current to flow in only one direction. Useful for rectification (converting AC to DC).Transistors: Three-terminal components (like BJTs or MOSFETs) that act as either amplifiers or switches. They are the fundamental building blocks of digital logic gates and microprocessors.Logic Gates: Digital circuits (AND, OR, NOT) that perform Boolean operations, serving as the foundation of computer processing.Explore the comprehensive Electrical Engineering Notes on Testbook for study material or check out the Electrical Technology Tutorials for detailed step-by-step
